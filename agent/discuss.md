The marketplace I would build

I would position it as:

A cultural commerce platform

Not:

Amazon for Egyptian products

Not:

another souvenir shop

Instead:

The marketplace for culturally meaningful products, experiences, and digital heritage.

The customer doesn't just buy a product.

They discover:

Story → Artifact → Culture → Product → Personalization → Experience

For example:

A user scans an ancient Egyptian inscription with Manetho.

Manetho identifies it.

The user learns that the inscription relates to a particular deity.

Then Manetho says:

“Explore products inspired by this story.”

The user sees:

jewelry
wall art
books
educational kits
3D models
clothing
digital art
personalized cartouches
museum replicas
home decor

And each product has the cultural story behind it.

That's considerably more defensible than a conventional marketplace.

3. The niche

My strongest recommendation:

AI-powered cultural commerce

More specifically:

Authenticated, story-driven heritage products and experiences.

The marketplace has four major pillars:

01 — Physical Heritage

Things people can buy.

02 — Digital Heritage

Things people can download/experience.

03 — Personalized Heritage

Products generated around the customer.

04 — Experiences

Things people can do rather than simply own.

That gives you a much larger TAM than souvenirs.

19. Marketplace naming

I would not automatically call it "Dark Pyramid Marketplace."

Dark Pyramid sounds like the technology company.

Manetho sounds like the cultural product.

The marketplace deserves its own brand.

Here are the directions I'd investigate.

Tier 1 — strongest

MANETHO MARKET

Simple.

Immediately connected to the existing brand.

Good if the marketplace is initially Ancient Egypt.

MANETHO

Then:

Manetho — Culture, Reimagined.

Marketplace becomes part of Manetho rather than a separate brand.

MANETHO HOUSE

Premium.

Feels like a cultural design house rather than a cheap souvenir store.

MANETHO COLLECTION

Very strong for premium products.

Discover the Collection.

Tier 2 — broader civilization brand

HERITAGE LAB

Technology + culture.

Could eventually expand globally.

CULTURA

Strong concept but likely trademark/domain challenges.

CHRONICLE

Excellent conceptual brand:

Own a piece of the story.

ORIGIN

Very premium.

Discover where stories begin.

ATLAS

Broad cultural discovery concept.

Tier 3 — Egyptian identity

KEMET

Very strong culturally.

Kemet references the ancient Egyptian name for Egypt.

Potential issue: enormous existing usage, so trademark/domain research is essential.

ANKH

Strong but extremely saturated.

PER ANKH

Very interesting.

Ancient Egyptian concept associated with the "House of Life."

Could work beautifully for an intellectual/cultural marketplace.

But trademark/domain clearance is essential.

SHEMSU

Potentially interesting, but requires careful linguistic/cultural validation.
