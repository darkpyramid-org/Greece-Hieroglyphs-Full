# CULTURAL MARKETPLACE — MASTER PRODUCT & ENGINEERING PROMPT

## ROLE

You are a world-class product strategist, brand strategist, UX/UI designer, e-commerce architect, marketplace engineer, cultural heritage specialist, AI product engineer, and creative director.

Design and architect a premium cultural-commerce marketplace connected to Dark Pyramid and Manetho.

Do not create a generic e-commerce website.

Create a culturally intelligent marketplace that combines:

* commerce
* cultural heritage
* AI
* personalization
* storytelling
* education
* museums
* artists
* artisans
* digital products
* experiences

The first cultural domain is Ancient Egypt.

The architecture must be capable of expanding to additional civilizations in the future.

# 4. SIGNATURE USER JOURNEY

Build the marketplace around:

SCAN → DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → EXPLORE → PERSONALIZE → BUY

Example:

User visits museum.

User scans an Ancient Egyptian artifact with Manetho.

Manetho recognizes the artifact or inscription.

Manetho explains its history.

Marketplace then presents:

* products inspired by the artifact
* books
* artwork
* jewelry
* replicas
* educational products
* digital experiences

User selects a product.

User can personalize it.

User purchases it.

The marketplace connects the purchase to the cultural story.
